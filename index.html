What if a seller does not turn up after token payment? ▼
All tokens paid through LandPin are held strictly inside our RERA-compliant ICICI Bank Nodal Escrow Vault. Funds are never released to the seller until biometric registry and physical cadastral possession are completed. If the seller defaults or fails to show up, 100% of the token is refunded within 48 hours.
How is my ₹5,00,000+ token advance protected? ▼
LandPin Escrow operates under Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the Real Estate Regulation Act. Each deposit requires three cryptographic authorizations: Buyer Consent, Advocate Title Clearance, and Escrow Trustee sign-off.
How do I contest plot boundary discrepancies? ▼
You can trigger an on-demand Cadastral Drone Survey through our Concierge Desk. A senior survey engineer will physically visit the site with RTK-GPS equipment to map boundary stones against official Karnataka Bhoomi revenue records.
Can I request an in-person advocate for registry? ▼
Yes! LandPin provides on-site Advocate attendance at all Bangalore Sub-Registrar offices (Indiranagar, Shivajinagar, Yelahanka, Bommanahalli, etc.) for deed vetting and immediate DD clearance.
